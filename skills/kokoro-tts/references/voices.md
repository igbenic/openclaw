# Kokoro voice notes

Common voices to try first:

- `af_heart`, warm default
- `af_nova`, cleaner and more professional
- `am_adam`, deeper male voice
- `bf_alice`, British female

If your deployed Kokoro image exposes a voices endpoint or ships a different set, prefer the server's advertised list over this file.

Practical rule:

- warm casual note: `af_heart`
- neutral assistant tone: `af_nova`
- lower male voice: `am_adam`
- British flavor: `bf_alice`
